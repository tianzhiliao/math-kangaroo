Q78 · Felix · Austria 2019 · #3 · 3 pts

Mother kangaroo and her son Max together weigh 60 kg (kilograms). The mother on her own weighs 52 kg. How heavy is Max?

{{o.mark}} {{o.text}} {{o.tag}}

× Not quite. The right answer is B, 8 kg.

Explanation · checked against the answer key

Mother and Max together weigh 60 kg. Mother alone weighs 52 kg.

To find Max, take Mother away: 60 − 52 = 8

Max weighs 8 kg. So the answer is B.
